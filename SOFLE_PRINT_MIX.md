# Sofle Print Mix — GammaCaps

Print mix for a Sofle v2 / Sofle Choc (58 keys: 4×6 matrix per half + 5 thumbs
per half), using the same row-sculpt logic as the KLP Lamé Sofle mix: tilted
variants on the two outermost rows, saddle home row, flat upper row.

## Mix per keyboard (58 caps)

| Qty | GammaCaps variant      | Goes on                                       |
| --: | :--------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
|  12 | Tilted Saddle           | Number row (tilt faces fingers)               |
|  12 | Normal                  | Upper row (above home)                        |
|  10 | Normal Saddle           | Home row (minus the two homing keys)          |
|   2 | Normal Saddle Homing    | F and J home positions                        |
|  12 | Tilted                  | Bottom row (rotate cap 180° so tilt faces up) |
|   8 | Thumb                   | Outer 1u thumb keys (4 per half)              |
|   2 | Thumb 1.25u or 1.5u     | Inner (widest) thumb key, one per half        |

The Sofle rotates its widest thumb key, so the choc families use the
rotated-stem `Thumb_90` wide variants; MX stems are symmetric and use the
plain wide thumbs.

## One-file production plates (JLC3DP / PCBWay)

Each switch family has the entire mix fused into one part (STL + zip) under
`Production/<family>/`: 60 caps — the 58-key mix plus both wide-thumb options
(2 × 1.25u and 2 × 1.5u), so one print covers either choice and the unused
pair becomes spares. Upload one file, order quantity 1, snip the connector
bars after printing.

| Family                | File                              | Plate size           |
| :-------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :------------------- |
| MX                    | `gammacap_MX_Sofle_Mix.stl`        | 152 × 161 × 9.9 mm   |
| Choc                  | `gammacap_Choc_Sofle_Mix.stl`      | 147 × 147 × 10.3 mm  |
| Choc (MX Spacing)     | `gammacap_Choc(MX)_Sofle_Mix.stl`  | 152 × 161 × 10.6 mm  |

Plate row map (top to bottom): Tilted Saddle ×8 | Tilted Saddle ×4 + Normal ×4 |
Normal ×8 | Normal Saddle ×8 | Normal Saddle ×2 + Homing ×2 + Tilted ×4 |
Tilted ×8 | Thumb ×8 | Thumb 1.25u ×2 + Thumb 1.5u ×2.

Plates are generated by `Tools/make_sofle_plate.py` and validated by
`Tools/tests/test_sofle_plate.py`: caps are decimated with a verified surface
error under 0.1mm (within SLA service tolerance) and fused with connector bars
welded into the cap skirts. Upstream meshes are all watertight and the wide
thumbs are native — no geometry surgery needed. Source STLs are exported Y-up;
the generator rotates them to Z-up.

Connector bars are a solid 3.0 × 3.0mm cross-section. JLC3DP's connected-parts
guideline requires every connection to be ≥1.5mm, and ≥3.0mm to be accepted as
one unified shell rather than flagged as loose small parts (their checker
otherwise rejects anything over 10 separate small parts). The 3.0mm bars clear
that bar; the 1.0mm bars used earlier were rejected. Choose **SLA or SLS** —
JLC's connected-parts method does not support MJF/FDM/WJP/BJ/SLM. After
printing, snip the bars with flush cutters and clean the nubs with a knife.
https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/213-Connected-Parts-Printing-Guide

Material guidance (community-tested on comparable 3D-printed caps — see
KLP-Lame-Keycaps/SOFLE_PRINT_MIX.md for sources): SLA in JLC Black Resin is
the safe order for connected plates; MJF PA12-HP nylon (black, never PA12S-HP)
feels best but powder tumbling may snap caps off the bars; skip sanding
finishes, they loosen stem fit.
